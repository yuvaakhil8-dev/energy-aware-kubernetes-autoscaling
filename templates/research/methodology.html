Node-level energy model

The model is an output metric, never a scaling input. For each active node:

P_node(u) = P_idle + (P_max - P_idle) · u        [W]
E_node    = P_node(u) · Δt · PUE + startup_energy [J]
u         = served requests on the node / node capacity in the tick

Dynamic energy is attributed to pods by CPU-use share; idle and startup energy by requested-resource share. The per-pod attribution must sum to node energy. The sensitivity analysis recomputes energy from the same raw active-node/utilisation data under low/nominal/high power and PUE 1.0–1.6. Scaling decisions do not depend on energy, so this is a valid post-hoc energy recalculation.
