How each strategy scales

Static

Keeps a fixed replica count regardless of load. Stable and predictable but can over-provision during light load and under-provision during heavy load.

HPA-style

Reacts to CPU utilisation. Verified runtime configuration: scale up at 80%, scale down at 30%, with a 5% hysteresis band and a 30–60 s cooldown to limit oscillation.

EAES

Adds energy-per-request and SLA awareness on top of CPU feedback. Scales up on SLA risk and sustained high CPU, and scales down only when SLA is safe and load has been low long enough.
