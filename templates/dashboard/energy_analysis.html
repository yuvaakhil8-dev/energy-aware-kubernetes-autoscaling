Measurement disclaimer

Energy figures on this page are estimates produced by the CPU-utilisation model P = P_idle + (P_max − P_idle) · CPU/100 with P_idle = 30 W, P_max = 80 W, interval Δt = 15 s and E_req = P · Δt / N_req. They are not direct power-meter readings. No claim of measured electrical savings is made.

Scientific interpretation

In the current 27-record local dataset, EAES does not show lower energy per request than Static or HPA in several cells. This is recorded honestly rather than adjusted. The dataset was generated under different throughput assumptions per strategy, so the energy-per-request values are not directly comparable across strategies without re-running the experiment with a normalised request count. Treat the figures as an illustration of the model, not as evidence of savings.
